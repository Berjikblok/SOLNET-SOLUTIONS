Energy Monitoring System

Biliran Province State University utilizes a robust and redundant power distribution system centered around three primary substations located on campus. To guarantee a reliable and stable electricity supply for its various facilities, BiPSU employs three separate 3-bank transformer configurations. This sophisticated electrical setup provides significant capacity and operational flexibility, ensuring that the critical power needs of the university are consistently met to support its academic and administrative operations.

Substations.

01

SUBSTATION 1 ENERGY MONITORING PLAN

Connects digital energy meters from its single-phase welding transformer and two 3-100kVA wye-delta banks directly to LAN Box 1, enabling centralized telemetry for the Automotive, Technology, Maritime, Ceramics buildings, and Sports Complex.

02

SUBSTATION 2 ENERGY MONITORING PLAN

Utilizes LAN Box 2 as a focal data aggregation point, linking smart meters across two 3-100 kVA wye-delta transformer banks to continuously monitor consumption in core academic facilities such as the Gymnasium, Student Center, ULRC, and Nursing buildings.

03

SUBSTATION 3 ENERGY MONITORING PLAN (NEW)

Feeds power consumption metrics from its 3-100 kVA wye-delta transformer bank into LAN Box 3, providing network visibility for residential and administrative facilities including BiPSU De Hotel, the LHS Building, Hostel, and Dormitory.

Implementation

Built for BiPSU.

This Energy Monitoring System is an implementation designed for Biliran Province State University (BiPSU) in Naval, Biliran. It covers the three campus substations shown above: the digital energy meters on each transformer bank are connected to LAN Box 1, LAN Box 2 and LAN Box 3, which gather the readings so the university can see how much power each building uses from one place. With this setup, BiPSU can track consumption per substation and per building, notice unusual loads early, and plan maintenance and energy-saving measures based on actual data, supporting a reliable power supply for its academic and administrative operations.
